What it holds

Shape the content, not the page.

You describe what a product, a post, or a landing page is made of. Inkling gives your team the screens to fill it in, and gives your sites one clean way to read it back.

FR 01

Your own shapes

Build the models your work actually has — repeating sections, image galleries, links between records. No field type you have to work around.

FR 02

A real edit history

Every save keeps what it replaced. Read an old version before you restore it, and put anything back without losing the thing you're replacing.

FR 03

Show work early

Send an unpublished piece to anyone with a link that opens for an hour and expires on its own. No account, no access to anything else.

FR 04

Move together

See who else is in a record while you're in it. Lists update as your colleagues work, so nobody edits a version that changed ten minutes ago.

FR 05

Publish on a schedule

Set the hour and step away. Anything that no longer fits its model comes back to review instead of going out broken.

FR 06

Roles that hold

Writers keep to their own drafts, editors publish, admins shape the model. Change someone's role and it takes effect on their next click.

What your sites read

One request, whole page.

Ask for a piece of content and the images and linked records come back filled in — not as references you have to go and fetch. Drafts never appear here, whoever asks.

Reel 02

The change arrives on its own.

When something goes live, your sites are told. No timer polling for news, no cache you have to guess the age of — the page that changed is the page that refreshes.

Live updates
Your sites hear the moment a piece is published, pulled, or replaced. They're told what changed, then read it back the same way they always do.
In the room
Names appear on a record while your colleagues have it open. Nobody discovers the collision at save time.
Working assistant
Bring your own assistant and it works on this content. Draft into a field, tighten a paragraph, write the summary, suggest titles — it can see your models, so it answers about your site instead of in general.
Answers for visitors
Turn on the public assistant and readers can ask questions of the page they're on. It answers from what you've published and says so plainly when the answer isn't there.
Many at once
Select a season's worth of posts and publish, archive, or retire them together. Each one is checked on its own, and you're told exactly which didn't pass.
Extendable
Add your own sections, screens, and content shapes as self-contained pieces. Switch one on and it's there — nothing restarts, nothing rebuilds.

Roll it.

Run it, open the admin, and start writing. The first person through the door becomes the owner.
