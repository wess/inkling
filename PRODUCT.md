# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Business owners and their teams maintaining client websites. Primary editors
may have no programming experience and need to recognize their actual pages.

## Product Purpose

Inkling lets a site's team update its content, images, catalog and page layout.
Success means a first-time editor can find the right thing, change it, preview
it and know whether the change reached the website.

## Operating Context

Editors work in the site's admin application and can ask Inky for guided changes.
An existing website supplies its own templates and content model.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The visual editor works with existing pages and guided controls. Editors click
text or images and can move or hide supported sections. Changes must use the
same validation, permissions and revision history as the ordinary editor.
Public instructions use example domains, never a client's test site.

## Product Principles

- Show the actual page beside the controls that change it.
- Keep preview separate from publishing and clearly show unsaved work.
- A failed save stays visible and keeps the editor's work intact.
- Use familiar task names and explain consequences before actions.
